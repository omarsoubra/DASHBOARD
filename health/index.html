Step 1 of 3

Share your daily activity with Omar

Once a day your iPhone sends Omar these daily totals from Apple Health (including your Apple Watch):

  • Steps
  • Active calories
  • Exercise minutes
  • Resting heart rate
  • Hours of sleep

Nothing else. No location, no workouts in detail, no other health records, no contacts or photos.

Why: so your calories and training are set from how active you really are, and Omar can spot when you're run down before it costs you progress.

Who sees it: only Omar, inside his coaching dashboard. It's stored in LOCKED IN's private, access-controlled database and is never sold or shared.

Your choice: you can stop at any time on this page, and everything already sent is deleted.

Step 3 of 3

Turn it on

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap LOCKED IN Health once. Tap Allow for everything it asks.
  2. Tap Automation at the bottom → + → Time of Day.
  3. Pick 9:00 PM, Daily, Run Immediately → Next → LOCKED IN Health.

✓ Apple Health connected

New phone? Do steps 2 and 3 again.
